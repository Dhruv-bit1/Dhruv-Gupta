Technician visit: S06 · 60 minutes

Why: After the 24 March battery swap, the GPS/logger UTC discrepancy persists; duplicate logger times overlap the restart. This compromises ordering and the interpretation of multiple weather channels. Second choice: S05 has extensive RH saturation and a persistent GPS installation mismatch, but its remaining weather-channel timing is intact.

0–10 min: Photograph UTC clock, GPS lock/status and power readings without resetting.
10–25: Compare raw NMEA UTC with logger UTC and timezone configuration; check firmware clock-source setting.
25–40: Inspect battery connections, reboot log and buffering/replay settings.
40–55: Take synchronized readings against an independent UTC clock and retain raw logs.
55–60: Record the offset, decide whether timestamp correction is independently demonstrable, and document follow-up.

Important incidents

S03: 481 values stuck at 6.5°C (18–21 Mar), plus isolated large spikes. Do not average the flatline into climate summary.
S05: 451 relative-humidity readings above 100%. Check sensor; do not clip to 100%. Valid NMEA positions systematically disagree with the installation sheet.
S08: 214 missing slots around 16–18 Mar and 144 extra repeated timestamps on 5 Apr. Neither can be repaired by naive interpolation.
S02: pressure step after 21 Mar board exchange is consistent with a calibration discontinuity; needs independent standard.
S04: large rain rates near funnel cleaning on 2 Apr; keep provisionally pending independent comparison.
S07: replacement anemometer brand may have a different transfer function. Verify calibration before comparisons.

Method / limitations. Automated range, continuity, persistence, abrupt-jump and NMEA consistency checks; one row in flags.csv represents an episode, not a count of corrupted samples. GPS void records are flagged on metadata only, not grounds to discard coincident weather. The station install sheet is a reference, not guaranteed correct. No replacement values are silently inserted. Thresholds are conservative heuristics; these are provisional station/channel decisions rather than full certified validation.
